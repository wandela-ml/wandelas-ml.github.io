Featured Projects

🏪 Tobhe – Business Oversight & Self-Service Checkout

Tobhe is a business oversight and self-service checkout ecosystem designed for Kenyan businesses. Its vision is to reduce queues, minimise human error, and give business owners better visibility into branch operations.

The deployed MVP provides functionality for branch operations, inventory management, customer orders, payment processing, and business oversight. It integrates Safaricom Daraja's M-Pesa Express API to initiate customer payment requests and process payment callbacks.

The broader product vision is a self-service experience where customers select items digitally, receive an M-Pesa PIN prompt on their phones, and collect verified orders after payment confirmation.

Status: MVP Deployed

Technologies: Python, SQLite, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Safaricom Daraja API.
