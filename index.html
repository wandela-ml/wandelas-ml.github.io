Featured Projects

🏪 Tobhe – Business Oversight Platform

Tobhe is a Python-based business oversight application designed to help Kenyan businesses manage branch operations, inventory, customer orders, and payments.

The deployed MVP provides branch operations, inventory management, customer order processing, and payment integration. It uses Safaricom Daraja's M-Pesa Express API to initiate customer payment requests and process payment callbacks.

The application connects business operations with payment processing, helping improve visibility and accountability across business branches.

Status: MVP Deployed

Technologies: Python, SQLite, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Safaricom Daraja API.

🎓 NafuLearn – AI-Powered CBC Learning

NafuLearn is an AI-powered learning platform designed around Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), currently focused on Grade 10 Computer Studies.

The project uses curriculum-based learning materials, document chunking, Gemini embeddings, and semantic retrieval to provide relevant learning context for AI-generated explanations.

Its development direction includes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), student answer evaluation, personalised feedback, retry functionality, and interactive Learn Mode.

Status: In Development

Technologies: Python, FastAPI, Google Gemini, ChromaDB, Embeddings, RAG.
